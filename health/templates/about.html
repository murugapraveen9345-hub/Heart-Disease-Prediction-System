How Our AI System Analyzes Cardiac Health

HeartGuard processes crucial cardiovascular biomarkers through an ensemble Gradient Boosting Machine classifier. Each vital sign is standardized and evaluated against thousands of historical diagnostic cases:

Blood Pressure & Serum Cholesterol

Evaluates resting systolic blood pressure alongside total serum cholesterol levels.

Max Heart Rate Achieved (Thalach)

Monitors cardiovascular endurance and oxygenation limits under physiological stress.

Chest Pain Type & Fasting Blood Sugar

Categorizes angina symptoms and metabolic indicators to provide early risk warnings.
